Give the table a clearer hierarchy:

- Column titles sit a step below the cells in quaternary ink, nested filter inputs included.
- The header and the rows have no rules. Rows sit inside the table's edge, and a hover, a selection or an expanded row shows as a rounded fill.
- Checkboxes have a hairline edge. On pointer devices, row checkboxes and expand chevrons show when the row is hovered or focused; once anything is selected, every checkbox shows.
- Selected rows take the brand tint.
- The selection actions float over the list as a raised, rounded bar, and the list leaves room under its last row for it.
- The footer's "select all" link uses the brand text colour.
- Expanded rows sit on a subtle inset panel, and group rows use a subtle rounded fill.
- Row cells use tabular figures.
- The empty state is a small icon over a short title and line, centred.
